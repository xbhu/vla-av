# Datasets

Datasets are **not stored in this repository**. Download each from its official source and place under the subdirectories below.

---

## Summary

| Dataset | Year | Domain | Scale | Modalities | Role in this repo | Priority |
|---------|------|--------|-------|------------|-------------------|----------|
| **nuScenes** | 2020 | Real (Boston / Singapore) | 1 k scenes, 20 s, 6 cams | RGB, LiDAR, Radar | Primary substrate — A1, A2, B1, C1–C4 | **Core** |
| **DriveLM-nuScenes** | 2024 | Real + Sim | 18 k scene graphs | RGB, Graph | Planning QA for A3 (memorization test); note QA is heavily templated | **Core** |
| **NAVSIM** | 2024 | Real (nuScenes-derived) | — | RGB, State | B2 consequence-aware evaluation (PDMS); pseudo-closed-loop, no rendering, much lighter than CARLA | **Core** |
| **CoVLA** | 2023 | Real (multi-source) | 80 k clips (30 s) | RGB video, State | A2 diversity supplement — richer and less templated than DriveLM | Optional |
| **Impromptu VLA** | 2025 | Real (multi-source) | 80 k clips (30 s) | RGB video, State | QA + trajectory tasks; potential diversity supplement | Optional |
| **NuInteract** | 2025 | Real (nuScenes) | 1 k scenes | RGB, LiDAR | Multi-turn QA; nuScenes-compatible, drop-in addition | Optional |
| **Bench2Drive / CARLA** | 2024 | Sim (CARLA) | 220 routes, 44 scenarios | RGB | B2 extended closed-loop; heavy (GPU rendering) — run on Roar Collab | Optional |

## Download

### nuScenes
Registration required: <https://www.nuscenes.org/nuscenes>

Place under:
```
datasets/raw/nuscenes/
```

### DriveLM-nuScenes
<https://github.com/OpenDriveLab/DriveLM>

Key file: `v1_1_train_nus.json`. Note the known image-path mismatch — see AutoVLA's preprocessing notes for the fix.

Place under:
```
datasets/raw/drivelm/
```
